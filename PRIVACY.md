# Privacy Policy for Darkroom Find

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Darkroom Find is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension operates completely offline and client-side on your local browser.

### 1. Data Collection & Transmission
Darkroom Find does NOT collect, transmit, track, or share any personal data, search queries, browsing history, or website content. No data ever leaves your device or browser.

### 2. Permissions Usage
- **storage**: Used exclusively to store user-defined local preferences (such as spotlight opacity, highlight glow colors, and per-site exclusion lists) locally on your device via `chrome.storage.sync`.
- **Host Permissions / Content Scripts**: Required solely to inject the non-destructive search overlay and highlight search matches directly on the active webpage upon pressing Ctrl+F. The extension does not read or transmit text beyond finding search terms in the visible tab.

### 3. Third-Party Services
This extension contains no third-party analytics, tracking scripts, external APIs, or advertisements.

### 4. Contact
If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
